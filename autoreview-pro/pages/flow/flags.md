# Flags

The Flags tab shows all failed actions based on your custom flag code.

To show the tab click the link at the top of the Flow Review tab:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Just lke the main [Flag Results](../flag-results.md) for the solution, but with the flags filtered by Area to the Flow.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (35).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

All the options are the same, including:

* FIlter
* Sort
* Quick Filter
* Edit Setting
* Add Setting
