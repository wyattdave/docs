# Exceptions

List of exceptions flagged in your flow

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (79).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Flows

Lists all flows within the solution, with&#x20;

* Trigger
* Complexity (See Review)
* Fails - total exceptions that are classified as fail
* Warnings - total exceptions that are classified as warnings

### Exceptions

Lists all exceptions, with:

* Flow - flow exception in
* Area - area of exception
* Value - the value from AutoReview that shows the error (this is dependent on the exception)
* Level - Fail or Warning
* Failed Reason - explains why it failed
